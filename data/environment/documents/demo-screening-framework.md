# Demonstration Environmental Screening Framework

This synthetic reference exists only to demonstrate governed retrieval, citations, laboratory validation, and human review. Its numerical values are fictional portfolio data and must not be used for regulatory, health, environmental, client, or project decisions.

Laboratory screening should preserve the original sample identifier, analyte, reported result, reporting limit, qualifier, and unit. A result may be compared with a criterion only when the units are identical or a deterministic conversion has been reviewed and tested.

A screening exceedance is an observation, not a conclusion about risk, remediation, compliance, or site classification. A qualified environmental practitioner must consider sampling design, data quality, exposure pathways, jurisdiction, land use, and the current approved regulatory framework.
